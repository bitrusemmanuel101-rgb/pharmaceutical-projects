
# Pharmaceutical Industry Projects

## Overview
This portfolio explores important challenges in the pharmaceutical industry through data analytics and statistical inference.

Our central research theme is antimicrobial resistance and antibiotic effectiveness.

The projects demonstrate how statistical methods can support evidence-based decisions in pharmaceutical research and public health.

## Project 1: Confidence Intervals
**Title:** Estimating Antibiotic Resistance Rates

**Objective:** Estimate the proportion of bacterial isolates resistant to selected antibiotics and quantify the uncertainty using confidence intervals.

**Techniques:** Sampling, proportions, standard error and confidence intervals.

[View Project 1](https://github.com/bitrusemmanuel101-rgb/antibiotic-resistance-confidence-intervals)

## Project 2: Hypothesis Testing
**Title:** Comparing Antibiotic Resistance Across Groups

**Objective:** Investigate whether resistance rates differ significantly across selected regions, bacterial species or time periods.

**Techniques:** Hypothesis testing, p-values and statistical significance.

[View Project 2](https://github.com/bitrusemmanuel101-rgb/antibiotic-resistance-hypothesis-testing)

## Project 3: A/B Testing
**Title:** Evaluating Antibiotic Treatment Outcomes

**Objective:** Analyse a randomised clinical trial to investigate whether a new treatment produces better outcomes than an existing standard treatment.

**Techniques:** Experimental design, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals and effect sizes.

[View Project 3]([https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/PROJECT-3-REPOSITORY](https://github.com/bitrusemmanuel101-rgb/antibiotic-clinical-trial-ab-testing))
